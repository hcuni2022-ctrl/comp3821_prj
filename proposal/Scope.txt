The format of the project will be mostly theoretical, and possibly with a bit scholar and experiment to help us better understand the problem and evaluate the results.

We intend to produce a few deliverables:
-Metrics to mathematically define a robustness/fragility of a path in our specific setting, or a relative robustness/fragility rating to guide path selection in our algorithm.
-Algorithm(s) to choose a path that minimise the total length and also stay robust if a failure occurs.
-Theoretical proof that algorithm behave as expected
-Documentation of all our progress
-A report to summary our major findings
-A poster

One preparatory by-product will be produce:
-Review of existing techniques as foundation. We will investigate the possibility to adapt them into our specific setting

A few possible extensions include:
-A comparison if we have multiple algorithm
-step-by-step analyse of specific case to check behaviour
-Empirical result and an graph experiment environment as by-product
-Study on extending our developed idea into mulit-k CTP setting

Here are a few boundaries we set for ourselves so that the work is manageable:
-Stochastic CTP will not be investigated, since it require deep understanding of probability and Bayesian decision
-Online decision will not be investigated, it will oversimplified the deterministic k-CTP into brute-force Dijkstra
-Adversary will not be introduce, it requires knowledge of game theory
